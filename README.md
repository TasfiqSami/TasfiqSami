About
I'm Tasfiq Ahmed, a software engineer, Minecraft network developer, and system administrator based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I build digital products and backend systems, develop custom Minecraft infrastructure, and work across the operational side of running reliable services.

My experience spans Minecraft development since 2020 and software development and system administration since 2021. I enjoy owning the complete engineering process: understanding the problem, shaping the architecture, building the product, integrating services, deploying it, and improving it through real usage.

I care about the details behind good software: clean architecture, thoughtful UX, security, measurable performance, and systems that are practical to maintain as they grow.
