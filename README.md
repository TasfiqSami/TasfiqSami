## About me

I'm **Tasfiq Ahmed Sami**, a software engineer and technology builder based in **Dhaka, Bangladesh**. I lead **[SYXLOR](https://syxlor.xyz)**, where I work across product direction, software development, digital platforms, and the infrastructure behind them.

My work brings together **full-stack development, backend engineering, Minecraft systems, and server infrastructure**. I enjoy taking an idea from an early concept to a working product: understanding the problem, shaping the architecture, building the experience, integrating services, deploying it, and improving it through real use.

I care about more than getting something to run. I value **maintainable code, thoughtful user experience, security, performance, and systems that can evolve without becoming difficult to manage**.
